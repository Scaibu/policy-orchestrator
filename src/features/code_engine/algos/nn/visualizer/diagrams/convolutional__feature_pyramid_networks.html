◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Feature Pyramid Network Multi-Scale Feature Construction
Require: Backbone stage activations $\{C_l\}_{l=2}^L$, lateral weights $\{W_{\text{lat}}^{(l)}, b_{\text{lat}}^{(l)}\}_{l=2}^L$, smoothing weights $\{W_{\text{smooth}}^{(l)}, b_{\text{smooth}}^{(l)}\}_{l=2}^L$, uniform channel dimension $d$.
Ensure: Multi-scale pyramid representations $\{P_l\}_{l=2}^L$ where $P_l \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times H_l \times W_l}$.
1 $M_L \gets \text{Conv1x1}(C_L, W_{\text{lat}}^{(L)}, b_{\text{lat}}^{(L)})$
2 for $l = L-1$ \textbf{downto} $2$ do
3 $C_{\text{proj}} \gets \text{Conv1x1}(C_l, W_{\text{lat}}^{(l)}, b_{\text{lat}}^{(l)})$
4 $M_{\text{up}} \gets \text{Upsample2x}(M_{l+1})$
5 $M_l \gets C_{\text{proj}} + M_{\text{up}}$
6 for $l = 2$ \textbf{to} $L$ do
7 $P_l \gets \text{Conv3x3Padding1}(M_l, W_{\text{smooth}}^{(l)}, b_{\text{smooth}}^{(l)})$
8 return $\{P_l\}_{l=2}^L$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Multi-Scale Semantic Receptive Field Preservation
\label{thm:receptive_field} Let $R(C_l)$ denote the effective receptive field (ERF) of backbone stage $C_l$. Under nearest-neighbor upsampling $\mathcal{U}_{2\times}$ and additive lateral fusion, every spatial unit $(i, j)$ in $M_l$ possesses an effective receptive field spanning: $$ R(M_l) = R(C_L) \quad \forall l \in \{2, \dots, L\} $$ thereby endowing fine-resolution spatial representations with global semantic contextual coverage.
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: Lateral $1 \times 1$ projections: $\mathcal{O}\left( \sum_{l=2}^L d \cdot C_l \cdot H_l \cdot W_l \right)$.
▸ Time Complexity: Nearest-neighbor upsampling: $\mathcal{O}\left( \sum_{l=2}^{L-1} d \cdot H_l \cdot W_l \right)$.
▸ Time Complexity: $3 \times 3$ smoothing convolutions: $\mathcal{O}\left( \sum_{l=2}^L 9 \cdot d^2 \cdot H_l \cdot W_l \right)$.
▸ Time Complexity: Total Time: Linear in spatial pixels $\mathcal{O}\left( d \sum_{l=2}^L (C_l + 9d) H_l W_l \right)$.
▸ Space Complexity: $\mathcal{O}\left( d \sum_{l=2}^L H_l W_l \right)$ auxiliary storage for $\{M_l, P_l\}$.
