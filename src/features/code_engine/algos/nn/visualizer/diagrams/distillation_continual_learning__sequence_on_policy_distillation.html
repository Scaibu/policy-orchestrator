◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Autoregressive Sequence Distillation Stepwise Loss
Require: Student logits $\mathbf{Z}^s \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times V}$, teacher logits $\mathbf{Z}^t \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times V}$, tokens $\mathbf{y} \in \{0, \dots, V-1\}^T$, mode $\in \{\text{forward\_kl}, \text{reverse\_kl}, \text{sequence\_sft}, \text{jsd}\}$, temperature $T_0$
Ensure: Token losses $\mathbf{L} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, mean loss $\bar{L}$, student entropies $\mathbf{H}^s \in \mathbb{R}^T$
1 Allocate $\mathbf{L} \in \mathbb{R}^T, \quad \mathbf{H}^s \in \mathbb{R}^T$
2 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$ do
3 $\mathbf{p}^s \gets \operatorname{Softmax}(\mathbf{Z}^s[t] / T_0), \quad \mathbf{p}^t \gets \operatorname{Softmax}(\mathbf{Z}^t / T_0)$
4 $\mathbf{H}^s[t] \gets - \sum_{v=0}^{V-1} \mathbf{p}^s[v] \ln(\max(10^{-12}, \mathbf{p}^s[v]))$
5 if $\text{mode} == \text{"reverse\_kl"}$ then
6 $\mathbf{L}[t] \gets \sum_{v=0}^{V-1} \mathbf{p}^s[v] \ln\left( \frac{\max(10^{-12}, \mathbf{p}^s[v])}{\max(10^{-12}, \mathbf{p}^t[v])} \right)$
7 else if $\text{mode} == \text{"sequence\_sft"}$ then
8 $\mathbf{L}[t] \gets - \ln(\max(10^{-12}, \mathbf{p}^s[\mathbf{y}[t]])))$
9 else if $\text{mode} == \text{"jsd"}$ then
10 $\mathbf{m} \gets 0.5 \cdot (\mathbf{p}^t + \mathbf{p}^s)$
11 $\mathbf{L}[t] \gets 0.5 \left( D_{\text{KL}}(\mathbf{p}^t \,\|\, \mathbf{m}) + D_{\text{KL}}(\mathbf{p}^s \,\|\, \mathbf{m}) \right)$
12 else
13 $\mathbf{L}[t] \gets \sum_{v=0}^{V-1} \mathbf{p}^t[v] \ln\left( \frac{\max(10^{-12}, \mathbf{p}^t[v])}{\max(10^{-12}, \mathbf{p}^s[v])} \right)$
14 $\bar{L} \gets \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} \mathbf{L}[t]$
15 return $(\mathbf{L}, \bar{L}, \mathbf{H}^s)$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Zero-Forcing Nature of Reverse KL Divergence
Let $p_t, p_s$ be discrete probability distributions over vocabulary $V$. If there exists a token index $v^* \in V$ such that $p_t(v^*) \to 0$, then: $$ \lim_{p_t(v^*) \to 0} D_{\text{KL}}(p_s \,\|\, p_t) = \infty \quad \text{whenever} \quad p_s(v^*) > 0 $$ thereby forcing any finite-loss student distribution to satisfy $p_s(v) = 0$ for all tokens where $p_t(v) = 0$.
Equivalence of On-Policy Reverse KL to Policy Gradient RL
Minimizing the on-policy sequence reverse KL divergence $\mathcal{L}_{\text{rev}}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}_{y \sim P_\theta}[\log P_\theta(y) - \log P_t(y)]$ is algebraically equivalent to maximizing policy gradient reinforcement learning with reward $R(y) = \log P_t(y) - \log P_\theta(y)$.
