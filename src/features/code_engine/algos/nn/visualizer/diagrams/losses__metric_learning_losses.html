◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Unified Metric Learning Loss Forward Evaluation
Require: Mode $\mathrm{mode} \in \{\text{triplet}, \text{arcface}, \text{cosface}\}$, Triplet tensors $(\mathbf{A}, \mathbf{P}, \mathbf{N})$ or Angular tensors $(\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{W}, \mathbf{y})$, margin $m \ge 0$, scale $s > 0$.
Ensure: Loss $\mathcal{L}$, auxiliary metrics.
1 if $\mathrm{mode} = \text{triplet}$ then
2 $L_{\mathrm{total}} \gets 0.0, \quad N_{\mathrm{active}} \gets 0$
3 for $i = 1$ to $B$ do
4 $d_{ap} \gets \sum_{k=1}^D (A_{i, k} - P_{i, k})^2, \quad d_{an} \gets \sum_{k=1}^D (A_{i, k} - N_{i, k})^2$
5 $val \gets d_{ap} - d_{an} + m$
6 if $val > 0$ then
7 $L_{\mathrm{total}} \gets L_{\mathrm{total}} + val, \quad N_{\mathrm{active}} \gets N_{\mathrm{active}} + 1$
8 return $\frac{L_{\mathrm{total}}}{B}, \quad N_{\mathrm{active}}$
9 else
10 Normalize each row of $\mathbf{W}$: $\hat{\mathbf{W}}_j \gets \frac{\mathbf{W}_j}{\|\mathbf{W}_j\|_2 + \epsilon}$
11 $L_{\mathrm{total}} \gets 0.0, \quad \mathbf{S} \gets []$
12 for $i = 1$ to $B$ do
13 $\hat{\mathbf{x}}_i \gets \frac{\mathbf{X}_i}{\|\mathbf{X}_i\|_2 + \epsilon}$
14 $\mathbf{cos\_row} \gets [\hat{\mathbf{x}}_i \cdot \hat{\mathbf{W}}_j]_{j=1}^C$
15 Clamp each $\cos\theta \in [-1, 1]$
16 $\mathbf{z}_i \gets [s \cdot \cos\theta_j]_{j=1}^C$
17 if $\mathrm{mode} = \text{arcface}$ then
18 $\theta_y \gets \arccos(\cos\theta_{y_i})$
19 $z_{i, y_i} \gets s \cdot \cos(\theta_y + m)$
20 else
21 $z_{i, y_i} \gets s \cdot (\cos\theta_{y_i} - m)$
22 $m_{\mathrm{max}} \gets \max_j z_{i, j}$
23 $\mathrm{lse} \gets m_{\mathrm{max}} + \log\sum_j \exp(z_{i, j} - m_{\mathrm{max}})$
24 $L_{\mathrm{total}} \gets L_{\mathrm{total}} + (\mathrm{lse} - z_{i, y_i})$
25 Append $\mathbf{z}_i$ to $\mathbf{S}$
26 return $\frac{L_{\mathrm{total}}}{B}, \quad \mathbf{S}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Exact Angular Margin Separation on $\mathbb{S}^{D-1}$
\label{thm:arcface_boundary} \textbf{Assumptions:} Let feature $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{S}^{D-1}$, normalized class weights $\mathbf{w}_1, \mathbf{w}_2 \in \mathbb{S}^{D-1}$, scale $s > 0$, and margin $m \in (0, \pi)$. \textbf{Guarantee:} ArcFace requires $\theta_1 + m < \theta_2$ to classify $\mathbf{x}$ as class 1, enforcing a minimal angular buffer of $m$ radians between classes. \textbf{Proof:} A sample is assigned to class 1 if and only if $z_1 > z_2 \iff s \cos(\theta_1 + m) > s \cos(\theta_2)$. Since $s > 0$ and cosine is strictly monotonically decreasing on $[0, \pi]$, $\cos(\theta_1 + m) > \cos(\theta_2) \iff \theta_1 + m < \theta_2 \implies \theta_2 - \theta_1 > m$. Thus the angular decision boundary is displaced by exactly $m$ radians away from class 1. \textbf{Limitations:} If $\theta_y + m > \pi$, the cosine function ceases to be monotonic, requiring threshold boundary clipping.
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(B \cdot D)$ for Triplet loss; $\mathcal{O}(B \cdot C \cdot D)$ for ArcFace/CosFace matrix multiplications and normalization.
▸ Space Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(B \cdot C)$ for cosine logit matrices.
