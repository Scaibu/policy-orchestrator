◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM FixMatch Confidence-Gated Consistency Loss
Require: Weak predictions $Q \in [0, 1]^{B \times K}$, Strong logits $Z \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times K}$, confidence threshold $\tau \in (0, 1)$.
Ensure: Pseudo-labels $\hat{y} \in \{-1, \dots, K-1\}^B$, masks $M \in \{0, 1\}^B$, sample losses $\ell \in \mathbb{R}^B$, mean loss $\mathcal{L}_u$.
1 Allocate $\hat{y} \gets -\mathbf{1}^B$, $M \gets \mathbf{0}^B$, $\ell \gets \mathbf{0}^B$
2 for $b \gets 1 \textbf{ to } B$ do
3 $p_{\text{max}} \gets \max_{k} Q_{b, k}$, $k^* \gets \arg\max_{k} Q_{b, k}$
4 if $p_{\text{max}} \ge \tau$ then
5 $M[b] \gets 1.0$, $\hat{y}[b] \gets k^*$
6 $z_{\text{max}} \gets \max_k Z_{b, k}$
7 $\text{lse} \gets z_{\text{max}} + \ln \left( \sum_k e^{Z_{b, k} - z_{\text{max}}} \right)$
8 $\ell[b] \gets -(Z_{b, k^*} - \text{lse})$
9 else
10 $M[b] \gets 0.0$, $\hat{y}[b] \gets -1$, $\ell[b] \gets 0.0$
11 $\mathcal{L}_u \gets \frac{1}{B} \sum_{b=1}^B \ell[b]$
12 return $\{\hat{y}, M, \ell, \mathcal{L}_u, \text{mean}(M)\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Confirmation Bias Bound under Hard Thresholding
Let $\epsilon_{\text{weak}}$ denote the error rate of weak predictions when $p_{\text{max}} \ge \tau$. The probability of incorporating a corrupted pseudo-label into the training gradient decays exponentially with the threshold gap: $$ \mathbb{P}(\hat{q}_b \ne y^* \mid p_{\text{max}} \ge \tau) \le \frac{1 - \tau}{\tau} \cdot \frac{\mathbb{P}(y \ne y^*)}{\mathbb{P}(y = y^*)} $$

▸ Argmax and LogSumExp: Takes $\mathcal{O}(K)$ FLOPs per sample.
▸ Total Time Complexity: $\Theta(B \cdot K)$ FLOPs.
▸ Storage: $\mathcal{O}(B)$ floats to store binary masks and sample loss vectors.
