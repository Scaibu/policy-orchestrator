Actor Critic Gae

In policy gradient optimization, raw Monte Carlo rollouts exhibit high variance, while 1-step bootstrapping introduces approximation bias. <strong>Actor-Critic architectures</strong> resolve this duality by maintaining two cooperating function approximators: \begin{enumerate} \item <strong>The Actor $\pi_{\boldsymbol{\theta</strong>}(a | s)$}: Parameterizes the policy controlling action execution. \item <strong>The Critic $V_{\boldsymbol{\phi</strong>}(s)$}: Parameterizes the state-value function estimating expected discounted return. \end{enumerate} The 1-step temporal difference (TD) residual of the critic is defined by: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\delta_t^V = r_t + \gamma (1 - d_t) V_{\boldsymbol{\phi}}(s_{t+1}) - V_{\boldsymbol{\phi}}(s_t)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Actor-Critic 1-Step TD Residual, GAE Advantage, and Loss Evaluation
Require: Action probs $\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, step rewards $\mathbf{r} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, values $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, next values $\mathbf{V}' \in \mathbb{R}^T$, terminal flags $\mathbf{d} \in \{0, 1\}^T$, discount $\gamma$, decay $\lambda$
Ensure: TD residuals $\boldsymbol{\delta} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, GAE advantages $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, value targets $\mathbf{R} \in \mathbb{R}^T$, actor loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{act}}$, critic loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{crit}}$
1 Initialize $\boldsymbol{\delta} \in \mathbb{R}^T$
2 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$} \Comment{Compute 1-Step TD Residuals do
3 $next\_val \gets \mathbf{d}[t] \;?\; 0.0 : \mathbf{V}'[t]$
4 $\boldsymbol{\delta}[t] \gets \mathbf{r}[t] + \gamma \cdot next\_val - \mathbf{V}[t]$
5 Initialize $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^T, \quad running\_a \gets 0.0, \quad decay \gets \gamma \cdot \lambda$
6 for $t = T-1$ \textbf{downto} $0$} \Comment{Recursive GAE Backward Accumulation do
7 $not\_done \gets \mathbf{d}[t] \;?\; 0.0 : 1.0$
8 $running\_a \gets \boldsymbol{\delta}[t] + decay \cdot not\_done \cdot running\_a$
9 $\mathbf{A}[t] \gets running\_a$
10 Initialize $\mathbf{R} \in \mathbb{R}^T$
11 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$} \Comment{Fitted Value Targets do
12 $\mathbf{R}[t] \gets \mathbf{A}[t] + \mathbf{V}[t]$
13 $act\_accum \gets 0.0, \quad crit\_accum \gets 0.0$
14 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$} \Comment{Evaluate Losses do
15 $act\_accum \gets act\_accum + \ln(\max(10^{-8}, \mathbf{p}[t])) \cdot \mathbf{A}[t]$
16 $diff \gets \mathbf{V}[t] - \mathbf{R}[t]$
17 $crit\_accum \gets crit\_accum + diff^2$
18 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{act}} \gets - act\_accum / T, \quad \mathcal{L}_{\text{crit}} \gets crit\_accum / T$
19 return $(\boldsymbol{\delta}, \mathbf{A}, \mathbf{R}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{act}}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{crit}})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Interpolation Spectrum of Generalized Advantage Estimation
Let $\delta_t^V = r_t + \gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t)$ and let $\widehat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}(\gamma, \lambda)} = \sum_{l=0}^\infty (\gamma \lambda)^l \delta_{t+l}^V$. Then: \begin{enumerate} \item For $\lambda = 0$, $\widehat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}}$ collapses to the 1-step TD advantage estimator: $$ \widehat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}(\gamma, 0)} = \delta_t^V = r_t + \gamma V(s_{t+1}) - V(s_t) $$ \item For $\lambda = 1$, $\widehat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}}$ collapses to the exact Monte Carlo baseline-subtracted return: $$ \widehat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}(\gamma, 1)} = \sum_{l=0}^\infty \gamma^l r_{t+l} - V(s_t) = G_t - V(s_t) $$ \end{enumerate}
Variance Reduction Bound of GAE
Assume that individual TD residuals $\delta_t^V$ have bounded variance $\operatorname{Var}(\delta_t^V) \le \sigma_\delta^2$ and are approximately uncorrelated. The variance of the GAE advantage estimator is bounded by: $$ \operatorname{Var}\left( \widehat{A}_t^{\text{GAE}} \right) \le \frac{\sigma_\delta^2}{1 - (\gamma \lambda)^2} $$ which decreases strictly monotonically as $\lambda \to 0$.
