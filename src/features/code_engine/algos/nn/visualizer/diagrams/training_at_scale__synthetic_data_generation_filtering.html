Synthetic Data Generation Filtering

As frontier foundation models consume the finite supply of high-quality human-authored internet text, pre-training and instruction-tuning increasingly rely on synthetic data generated by teacher LLMs. However, unfiltered synthetic corpora cause catastrophic <strong>Model Collapse</strong> (Shumailov et al., 2023), characterized by progressive variance reduction, tail event extinction, and hallucination amplification. A synthetic candidate dataset $\mathcal{C} = \{ (x_k, s_k, \ell_k) \}_{k=1}^N$ comprises text strings $x_k$, reward/verifier quality scores $s_k \in [0, 1]$, and sequence token lengths $\ell_k \in \mathbb{N}$. The <strong>Tri-Stage Synthetic Filtering Pipeline</strong> filters candidates through a strict admission operator $\mathcal{A}: \mathcal{C} \to \{0, 1\}$: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\mathcal{A}(x_k) = \mathbf{1}\left( s_k \ge \tau_{\text{quality}} \right) \cdot \mathbf{1}\left( L_{\min} \le \ell_k \le L_{\max} \right) \cdot \prod_{x_j \in \mathcal{P}_{\text{accepted}}} \mathbf{1}\left( \mathcal{J}(W(x_k), W(x_j)) < \tau_{\text{dedup}} \right)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Synthetic Data Quality Filtering and Deduplication
Require: Candidates $\mathcal{C}$, min quality $\tau_q$, length bounds $[L_{\min}, L_{\max}]$, Jaccard threshold $\tau_{\text{dedup}}$
Ensure: Accepted samples $\mathcal{P}$, rejection breakdown $\mathcal{R}$, acceptance ratio $\mathcal{A}_{\text{ratio}}$
1 Initialize $\mathcal{P} \gets [\,], \quad \mathcal{W} \gets [\,]$
2 Initialize $\mathcal{R} \gets \{ \text{"low\_quality"}: 0, \, \text{"length\_violation"}: 0, \, \text{"duplicate"}: 0 \}$
3 for \textbf{each} sample $x \in \mathcal{C}$ do
4 Extract score $s \gets x.\text{quality\_score}$, length $\ell \gets x.\text{token\_count}$, text $t \gets x.\text{text}$
5 if $s < \tau_q$ then
6 $\mathcal{R}[\text{"low\_quality"}] \gets \mathcal{R}[\text{"low\_quality"}] + 1$
7 \textbf{continue}
8 if $\ell < L_{\min}$ \textbf{or} $\ell > L_{\max}$ then
9 $\mathcal{R}[\text{"length\_violation"}] \gets \mathcal{R}[\text{"length\_violation"}] + 1$
10 \textbf{continue}
11 $W_{\text{cand}} \gets \operatorname{Set}(\operatorname{lower}(\operatorname{split}(t)))$
12 $\text{is\_dup} \gets \textbf{false}$
13 for \textbf{each} $W_{\text{prev}} \in \mathcal{W}$ do
14 $J \gets |W_{\text{cand}} \cap W_{\text{prev}}| / |W_{\text{cand}} \cup W_{\text{prev}}|$
15 if $J \ge \tau_{\text{dedup}}$ then
16 $\text{is\_dup} \gets \textbf{true}$
17 \textbf{break}
18 if $\text{is\_dup}$ then
19 $\mathcal{R}[\text{"duplicate"}] \gets \mathcal{R}[\text{"duplicate"}] + 1$
20 else
21 Append $x$ to $\mathcal{P}$
22 Append $W_{\text{cand}}$ to $\mathcal{W}$
23 $\mathcal{A}_{\text{ratio}} \gets |\mathcal{P}| / |\mathcal{C}|$
24 return $(\mathcal{P}, \mathcal{R}, \mathcal{A}_{\text{ratio}}, |\mathcal{C}|)$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Metric Property of the Jaccard Distance Operator
Let $\mathcal{U}$ be a finite vocabulary, and let $\mathcal{P}(\mathcal{U}) \setminus \{\emptyset\}$ be the power set of non-empty word sets. The Jaccard distance function $d_{\mathcal{J}}(A, B) = 1 - \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$ is a valid mathematical metric: \begin{enumerate} \item $d_{\mathcal{J}}(A, B) \ge 0$, and $d_{\mathcal{J}}(A, B) = 0 \iff A = B$ \item $d_{\mathcal{J}}(A, B) = d_{\mathcal{J}}(B, A)$ \item $d_{\mathcal{J}}(A, C) \le d_{\mathcal{J}}(A, B) + d_{\mathcal{J}}(B, C)$ \quad (Triangle Inequality) \end{enumerate}
Model Collapse Prevention via Quality-Thresholded Filtering
Let true data distribution $p^*(x)$ have support $\mathcal{X}$. Suppose a generation model $p_\theta(x)$ has density support $\mathcal{X}_\theta$, where low-quality and hallucinated samples occupy subset $\mathcal{X}_{\text{bad}} \subset \mathcal{X}_\theta$ with quality score $s(x) < \tau_{\text{quality}}$. If synthetic training data is collected without filtering, the probability mass on valid distributions degrades exponentially under repeated recursive training: $p^{(k)}(\mathcal{X}_{\text{bad}}) \to 1$. Under the admission filter $\mathcal{A}(x) = \mathbf{1}(s(x) \ge \tau_{\text{quality}})$, the corrupted mass is identically zero: $$ \int_{\mathcal{X}_{\text{bad}}} p_{\text{admitted}}(x) \, dx \equiv 0 $$ thereby eliminating catastrophic recursive drift into low-quality hallucination states.
