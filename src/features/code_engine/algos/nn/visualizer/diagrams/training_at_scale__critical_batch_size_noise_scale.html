◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Critical Batch Size and Gradient Noise Scale Estimation
Require: Per-sample gradients $\mathbf{g} \in \mathbb{R}^{B_{\text{sample}} \times P}$, step $t$, total steps $T$, bounds $[B_{\min}, B_{\max}]$
Ensure: Noise scale $B_{\text{noise}}$, covariance trace $\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma)$, gradient norm $\|G\|^2$, recommended batch size $B^*$, efficiency $\mathcal{E}$
1 $B_{\text{sample}} \gets \operatorname{len}(\mathbf{g}), \quad P \gets \operatorname{len}(\mathbf{g}[0])$
2 Compute sample mean gradient: $\bar{\mathbf{G}} \gets \frac{1}{B_{\text{sample}}} \sum_{i=1}^{B_{\text{sample}}} \mathbf{g}_i$
3 Compute squared mean norm: $\|\bar{\mathbf{G}}\|^2 \gets \sum_{j=1}^P \bar{G}_j^2$
4 Initialize trace sum $\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma) \gets 0.0$
5 for $j = 0$ \textbf{to} $P - 1$ do
6 $\operatorname{Var}_j \gets \frac{1}{B_{\text{sample}} - 1} \sum_{i=1}^{B_{\text{sample}}} (g_{i, j} - \bar{G}_j)^2$
7 $\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma) \gets \operatorname{tr}(\Sigma) + \operatorname{Var}_j$
8 $B_{\text{noise}} \gets \operatorname{tr}(\Sigma) / \max(10^{-12}, \|\bar{\mathbf{G}}\|^2)$
9 $\text{progress} \gets \min(1.0, \max(0.0, t / T))$
10 $B^* \gets \operatorname{clip}\left( \operatorname{round}\left( B_{\text{noise}} \cdot (0.5 + 0.5 \cdot \text{progress}) \right), B_{\min}, B_{\max} \right)$
11 $\mathcal{E} \gets 1.0 / (1.0 + B^* / \max(1.0, B_{\text{noise}}))$
12 return $(B_{\text{noise}}, \operatorname{tr}(\Sigma), \|\bar{\mathbf{G}}\|^2, B^*, \mathcal{E})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Unbiased Estimation of Covariance Trace from Mini-Batch Gradients
Let $\{g_1, \dots, g_B\}$ be $B \ge 2$ independent and identically distributed per-sample gradient vectors drawn from distribution $\mathcal{D}$ with mean $G = \mathbb{E}[g_i]$ and covariance $\Sigma = \operatorname{Cov}(g_i)$. Define the sample mean $\bar{g} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B g_i$ and the sample variance estimator for coordinate $j \in \{1, \dots, P\}$: $$ s_j^2 = \frac{1}{B - 1} \sum_{i=1}^B \left( g_{i, j} - \bar{g}_j \right)^2 $$ Then the sample trace $\operatorname{tr}(\hat{\Sigma}) = \sum_{j=1}^P s_j^2$ is an unbiased estimator of the population trace $\operatorname{tr}(\Sigma)$: $$ \mathbb{E}\left[ \operatorname{tr}(\hat{\Sigma}) \right] = \operatorname{tr}(\Sigma) $$
Pareto Optimality of Critical Batch Size Schedule
Under a quadratic loss landscape $\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \frac{1}{2} \theta^\top H \theta$ with learning rate $\eta(B) = \frac{\eta_0}{1 + B_{\text{noise}} / B}$, the step progress per unit of wall-clock time $\mathcal{R}_{\text{time}}(B)$ and per unit of total compute tokens $\mathcal{R}_{\text{FLOPs}}(B)$ cannot be simultaneously increased beyond $B = B_{\text{noise}}$. Specifically, the Pareto frontier satisfies: $$ \frac{d \mathcal{S}}{d B} \cdot \frac{B}{\mathcal{S}} = -\frac{B_{\text{noise}}}{B + B_{\text{noise}}} $$ which reaches elasticity $-0.5$ at $B = B_{\text{noise}}$.
