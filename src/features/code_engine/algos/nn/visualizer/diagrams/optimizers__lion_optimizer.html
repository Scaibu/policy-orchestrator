Lion Optimizer

\begin{definition}[Lion Optimizer Update Rule] Given initial parameter vector $\theta_0 \in \mathbb{R}^P$, initial momentum $m_0 = \mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, and hyperparameters $\eta > 0, \lambda \ge 0, \beta_1, \beta_2 \in [0, 1)$, the recurrence relations for step $t \ge 1$ and coordinate $i \in \{1, \dots, P\}$ are: \end{definition}
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\min_{t=1, \dots, T} \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{P} \|\nabla \mathcal{L}(\theta_t)\|_1\right] \le \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{T^{1/4}}\right)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Lion (EvoLved Sign Momentum) Step Execution
Require: Parameter vector $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^P$, gradient vector $g \in \mathbb{R}^P$, momentum buffer $m \in \mathbb{R}^P$
Require: Step size $\eta > 0$, decays $\beta_1, \beta_2 \in [0, 1)$, weight decay $\lambda \ge 0$
Ensure: Updated parameters $\theta_{\text{next}} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, updated momentum buffer $m_{\text{next}} \in \mathbb{R}^P$
1 $P \gets \text{length}(\theta)$
2 if $P = 0$ \textbf{or} $\text{length}(g) \ne P$ \textbf{or} $\text{length}(m) \ne P$ then
3 \textbf{throw} PreconditionFailureException(``Buffer dimensions mismatch or empty'')
4 if $\eta \le 0$ \textbf{or} $\lambda < 0$ \textbf{or} $\beta_1 < 0$ \textbf{or} $\beta_1 \ge 1$ \textbf{or} $\beta_2 < 0$ \textbf{or} $\beta_2 \ge 1$ then
5 \textbf{throw} PreconditionFailureException(``Hyperparameter domain violation'')
6 $\theta_{\text{next}} \gets \text{new Array of size } P$
7 $m_{\text{next}} \gets \text{new Array of size } P$
8 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } P$ do
9 $c_i \gets \beta_1 \cdot m[i] + (1.0 - \beta_1) \cdot g[i]$
10 $s_i \gets \begin{cases} 1.0 & \text{if } c_i > 0.0 \\ -1.0 & \text{if } c_i < 0.0 \\ 0.0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
11 $\theta_{\text{next}}[i] \gets \theta[i] \cdot (1.0 - \eta \cdot \lambda) - \eta \cdot s_i$
12 $m_{\text{next}}[i] \gets \beta_2 \cdot m[i] + (1.0 - \beta_2) \cdot g[i]$
13 return $\{\text{updated\_parameters}: \theta_{\text{next}}, \text{updated\_exp\_avg}: m_{\text{next}}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Convergence of Sign-SGD / Lion under Smooth Objectives
Let $\mathcal{L}: \mathbb{R}^P \to \mathbb{R}$ be $L$-smooth. Assume gradients have bounded coordinate noise $\mathbb{E}[|g_{t, i} - \nabla_i \mathcal{L}(\theta_t)|^2] \le \sigma^2$. Under an appropriately chosen step-size schedule $\eta_t = \frac{\eta_0}{\sqrt{t}}$ and momentum decay, the expected $L_1$-norm of the objective gradient converges as: $$ \min_{t=1, \dots, T} \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{P} \|\nabla \mathcal{L}(\theta_t)\|_1\right] \le \mathcal{O}\left(\frac{1}{T^{1/4}}\right) $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ FLOPs per Parameter: Each coordinate requires 2 multiply-adds (for $c$ and $m$), 1 comparison (for sign), and 1 multiply-subtract (for $\theta$).
▸ Total Time: $\Theta(P)$ FLOPs per step (significantly faster than Adam since no square root or division is performed).
▸ Auxiliary Memory: Exactly one persistent vector $m \in \mathbb{R}^P$ ($4P$ bytes in FP32 vs. $8P$ for Adam).
▸ Total Auxiliary Space: $\Theta(P)$ floats (50\% memory reduction relative to Adam).
