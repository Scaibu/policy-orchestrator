Scaled Dot Product Attention

Let $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_q = \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_k}$, $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_k = \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_k}$, $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_v = \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_v}$, and $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}} = (\mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty\})^{N_q \times N_k}$. The <strong>Scaled Dot-Product Attention</strong> operator is the parameterized nonlinear mapping: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\operatorname{Attn}: \boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_q \times \boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_k \times \boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_v \times \boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}} \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_v}$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Scaled Dot-Product Attention Forward Execution
Require: Query $\mathbf{Q} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_k}$, Key $\mathbf{K} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_k}$, Value $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_v}$
Require: Optional additive mask $\mathbf{M} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times N_k}$, optional scale factor $\tau \in \mathbb{R}_{> 0}$
Ensure: Output matrix $\mathbf{O} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_v}$, attention weights $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times N_k}$, scale factor $\tau$
1 $N_q \gets \text{rows}(\mathbf{Q}), \quad N_k \gets \text{rows}(\mathbf{K}), \quad d_k \gets \text{cols}(\mathbf{Q}), \quad d_v \gets \text{cols}(\mathbf{V})$
2 if $\tau \text{ is None or } \tau \le 0$ then
3 $\tau \gets 1.0 / \sqrt{d_k}$
4 $\mathbf{A} \gets \text{empty matrix of shape } (N_q, N_k)$
5 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } N_q$ do
6 $\mathbf{s}_i \gets \text{array of size } N_k$
7 for $j \gets 1 \textbf{ to } N_k$ do
8 $\text{dot} \gets \sum_{d=1}^{d_k} Q_{i, d} \cdot K_{j, d}$
9 $\text{score} \gets \text{dot} \cdot \tau$
10 if $\mathbf{M} \text{ is present}$ then
11 $\text{score} \gets \text{score} + M_{i, j}$
12 $s_{i}[j] \gets \text{score}$
13 $m_i \gets \max_{1 \le j \le N_k} s_i[j]$
14 $\text{exp\_sum} \gets \sum_{j=1}^{N_k} \exp(s_i[j] - m_i)$
15 for $j \gets 1 \textbf{ to } N_k$ do
16 if $\text{exp\_sum} > 0$ then
17 $A_{i, j} \gets \exp(s_i[j] - m_i) / \text{exp\_sum}$
18 else
19 $A_{i, j} \gets 1.0 / N_k$ \Comment{Uniform fallback for degenerate rows}
20 $\mathbf{O} \gets \text{zeros of shape } (N_q, d_v)$
21 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } N_q$ do
22 for $j \gets 1 \textbf{ to } N_k$ do
23 for $d \gets 1 \textbf{ to } d_v$ do
24 $O_{i, d} \gets O_{i, d} + A_{i, j} \cdot V_{j, d}$
25 return $\{\text{output}: \mathbf{O}, \text{attention\_weights}: \mathbf{A}, \text{scale\_factor}: \tau\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Unit Variance Preservation under Gaussian Components
Let coordinates $Q_{i, d} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ and $K_{j, d} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$ be mutually independent identically distributed standard normal random variables with zero mean and unit variance. Then the unscaled dot product $Z = \sum_{d=1}^{d_k} Q_{i, d} K_{j, d}$ has mean $\mathbb{E}[Z] = 0$ and variance $\operatorname{Var}(Z) = d_k$. Scaling by $\tau = 1/\sqrt{d_k}$ guarantees: $$ \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{Z}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right] = 0 \quad \text{\textbf{and}} \quad \operatorname{Var}\left(\frac{Z}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) = 1.0 $$
Convex Hull Containment
For any row-stochastic attention weight vector $\mathbf{a}_i \in \boldsymbol{\Delta}^{N_k - 1}$ and value vectors $\{\mathbf{v}_j\}_{j=1}^{N_k} \subset \mathbb{R}^{d_v}$, the output vector $\mathbf{o}_i = \sum_{j=1}^{N_k} A_{i, j} \mathbf{v}_j$ strictly resides within the convex hull: $$ \mathbf{o}_i \in \operatorname{conv}(\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_{N_k}) $$ Consequently, under any vector norm $\|\cdot\|$, $\|\mathbf{o}_i\| \le \max_{1 \le j \le N_k} \|\mathbf{v}_j\|$.
Shift Invariance of the Softmax Normalization
For any logit vector $\mathbf{s} \in \mathbb{R}^N$ and any scalar shift constant $c \in \mathbb{R}$: $$ \operatorname{softmax}(\mathbf{s} - c \mathbf{1}) \equiv \operatorname{softmax}(\mathbf{s}) $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: Computing inner products $\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^\top$: $N_q N_k d_k$ multiplications and additions ($\Theta(N_q N_k d_k)$ FLOPs).
▸ Time Complexity: Softmax evaluation: $N_q N_k$ scaling, max-finding, exp, and division operations ($\Theta(N_q N_k)$ FLOPs).
▸ Time Complexity: Value weighting $\mathbf{A}\mathbf{V}$: $N_q N_k d_v$ multiplications and additions ($\Theta(N_q N_k d_v)$ FLOPs).
▸ Time Complexity: Total Time: $\Theta(N_q N_k(d_k + d_v))$ FLOPs.
▸ Space Complexity: Attention weights buffer $\mathbf{A}$: $\Theta(N_q N_k)$ floats.
▸ Space Complexity: Output matrix $\mathbf{O}$: $\Theta(N_q d_v)$ floats.
▸ Space Complexity: Total Auxiliary Space: $\Theta(N_q N_k + N_q d_v)$.
