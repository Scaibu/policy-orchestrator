◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Affine Coupling Normalizing Flow Forward and Inverse Evaluation
Require: Vector $\mathbf{u} \in \mathbb{R}^D$, Weights $\mathbf{W}_s, \mathbf{b}_s, \mathbf{W}_t, \mathbf{b}_t$, Split $d$, Mode $\text{inverse} \in \{\text{true}, \text{false}\}$
Ensure: Output vector $\mathbf{v} \in \mathbb{R}^D$, Log-det Jacobian $\mathcal{J}$, Log-likelihood $\mathcal{L}$
1 $\mathbf{u}_1 \gets \mathbf{u}[1 \dots d], \quad \mathbf{u}_2 \gets \mathbf{u}[d+1 \dots D]$
2 $D_{\text{rem}} \gets D - d$
3 $\mathbf{s} \gets \text{zeros}(D_{\text{rem}}), \quad \mathbf{t} \gets \text{zeros}(D_{\text{rem}})$
4 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } D_{\text{rem}}$ do
5 $s_{\text{act}} \gets b_{s, i} + \sum_{j=1}^d W_{s, i, j} \cdot u_{1, j}$
6 $t_{\text{act}} \gets b_{t, i} + \sum_{j=1}^d W_{t, i, j} \cdot u_{1, j}$
7 $s_i \gets 2.0 \cdot \tanh(0.5 \cdot s_{\text{act}})$ \Comment{Clamped scale}
8 $t_i \gets t_{\text{act}}$
9 $\mathbf{v} \gets \text{copy}(\mathbf{u}_1)$
10 if $\text{inverse} = \text{false}$ then
11 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } D_{\text{rem}}$ do
12 Append $u_{2, i} \cdot \exp(s_i) + t_i$ to $\mathbf{v}$
13 $\mathcal{J} \gets \sum_{i=1}^{D_{\text{rem}}} s_i$
14 else
15 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } D_{\text{rem}}$ do
16 Append $(u_{2, i} - t_i) \cdot \exp(-s_i)$ to $\mathbf{v}$
17 $\mathcal{J} \gets -\sum_{i=1}^{D_{\text{rem}}} s_i$
18 $\mathbf{z}_{\text{eval}} \gets \mathbf{u} \text{ if not inverse else } \mathbf{v}$
19 $\text{sum\_sq} \gets \sum_{k=1}^D z_{\text{eval}, k}^2$
20 $\log p_z \gets -0.5 \cdot (D \cdot \ln(2\pi) + \text{sum\_sq})$
21 $\mathcal{L} \gets \log p_z - \mathcal{J}$
22 return $\{\text{output}: \mathbf{v}, \text{log\_det\_jacobian}: \mathcal{J}, \text{log\_likelihood}: \mathcal{L}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Change of Variables for Probability Density Functions
Let $\mathbf{Z} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ be a random vector with continuous probability density $p_Z(\mathbf{z})$, and let $\mathbf{X} = f(\mathbf{Z})$ where $f: \mathbb{R}^D \to \mathbb{R}^D$ is a diffeomorphism (smooth bijection with smooth inverse). Then the probability density of $\mathbf{X}$ is given by: $$ p_X(\mathbf{x}) = p_Z(f^{-1}(\mathbf{x})) \cdot \left| \det \left( \frac{\partial f^{-1}(\mathbf{x})}{\partial \mathbf{x}} \right) \right| $$
