◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM EWC Quadratic Penalty and Gradient Evaluation
Require: Current parameters $\mathbf{\theta} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, optimal old parameters $\mathbf{\theta}_A^* \in \mathbb{R}^P$, diagonal Fisher $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, penalty scale $\lambda$, task B loss $\mathcal{L}_B$
Ensure: Total loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}$, penalty $\mathcal{P}$, gradient vector $\mathbf{g} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, maximum drift $D_{\max}$
1 Allocate $\mathbf{g} \in \mathbb{R}^P, \quad \text{sum\_sq} \gets 0.0, \quad D_{\max} \gets 0.0$
2 for $i = 0$ \textbf{to} $P-1$ do
3 $\Delta_i \gets \mathbf{\theta}[i] - \mathbf{\theta}_A^*[i]$
4 $D_{\max} \gets \max(D_{\max}, |\Delta_i|)$
5 $\text{sum\_sq} \gets \text{sum\_sq} + \mathbf{F}[i] \cdot (\Delta_i)^2$
6 $\mathbf{g}[i] \gets \lambda \cdot \mathbf{F}[i] \cdot \Delta_i$
7 $\mathcal{P} \gets 0.5 \cdot \lambda \cdot \text{sum\_sq}$
8 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} \gets \mathcal{L}_B + \mathcal{P}$
9 return $(\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}}, \mathcal{P}, \mathbf{g}, D_{\max})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Bayesian Derivation via Laplace Approximation
Let $p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D}_A)$ be the posterior parameter distribution after observing dataset $\mathcal{D}_A$. Under a second-order Taylor expansion around maximum a posteriori mode $\theta_A^*$, $\log p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D}_A)$ is approximated by a multivariate Gaussian whose negative log-likelihood equals the EWC quadratic penalty: $$ -\log p(\theta \mid \mathcal{D}_A) \approx \text{const} + \frac{1}{2} (\theta - \theta_A^*)^T \mathcal{H}_A (\theta - \theta_A^*) \approx \text{const} + \frac{1}{2} \sum_{i=1}^P F_i (\theta_i - \theta_{A, i}^*)^2 $$
Bounded Task A Loss Degradation Under EWC Spring Constraint
Let $\theta_A^*$ be a local minimum of task $A$ loss $\mathcal{L}_A(\theta)$ with Hessian $\mathcal{H}_A \succeq 0$. If the parameter drift satisfies $\sum_{i=1}^P F_i (\theta_i - \theta_{A, i}^*)^2 \le \epsilon^2$, then the performance degradation on task $A$ is strictly bounded by: $$ \mathcal{L}_A(\theta) - \mathcal{L}_A(\theta_A^*) \le \frac{1}{2} \epsilon^2 + \mathcal{O}(\|\theta - \theta_A^*\|^3) $$
