◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM 2D Discrete Cross-Correlation Convolution Forward Pass
Require: Input $X \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times H_{\text{in}} \times W_{\text{in}}}$, weights $W \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times C_{\text{in}} \times K_h \times K_w}$, bias $b \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}}}$, strides $(s_h, s_w)$, padding $(p_h, p_w)$.
Ensure: Convolved tensor $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times H_{\text{out}} \times W_{\text{out}}}$, dimensions $H_{\text{out}}, W_{\text{out}}$.
1 $H_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor (H_{\text{in}} + 2p_h - K_h) / s_h \rfloor + 1$, $W_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor (W_{\text{in}} + 2p_w - K_w) / s_w \rfloor + 1$
2 Construct zero-padded tensor $X_{\text{pad}} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times (H_{\text{in}}+2p_h) \times (W_{\text{in}}+2p_w)}$
3 Allocate $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times H_{\text{out}} \times W_{\text{out}}}$
4 for $c_{\text{out}} \gets 0 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{out}} - 1$ do
5 for $h \gets 0 \textbf{ to } H_{\text{out}} - 1$ do
6 $h_{\text{start}} \gets h \cdot s_h$
7 for $w \gets 0 \textbf{ to } W_{\text{out}} - 1$ do
8 $w_{\text{start}} \gets w \cdot s_w$
9 $\text{acc} \gets b[c_{\text{out}}]$
10 for $c_{\text{in}} \gets 0 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{in}} - 1$ do
11 for $i \gets 0 \textbf{ to } K_h - 1$ do
12 for $j \gets 0 \textbf{ to } K_w - 1$ do
13 $\text{acc} \gets \text{acc} + W[c_{\text{out}}][c_{\text{in}}][i][j] \cdot X_{\text{pad}}[c_{\text{in}}][h_{\text{start}} + i][w_{\text{start}} + j]$
14 $Y[c_{\text{out}}][h][w] \gets \text{acc}$
15 return $\{Y, H_{\text{out}}, W_{\text{out}}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Translation Equivariance of 2D Convolution
Let $T_{\Delta}$ denote the spatial translation operator $(T_{\Delta} X)(c, y, x) = X(c, y - \Delta_y, x - \Delta_x)$ over the continuous plane $\mathbb{R}^2$ with stride $s_h = s_w = 1$. Then 2D convolution $\Phi_W$ commutes with translation: $$ \Phi_W(T_{\Delta} X) = T_{\Delta}(\Phi_W(X)) $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Multiply-Accumulate Operations: For each output cell, $C_{\text{in}} K_h K_w$ multiplications and additions are performed.
▸ Total Time Complexity: $\Theta(C_{\text{out}} \cdot H_{\text{out}} \cdot W_{\text{out}} \cdot C_{\text{in}} \cdot K_h \cdot K_w)$ FLOPs.
▸ Activation Storage: $\mathcal{O}(C_{\text{out}} \cdot H_{\text{out}} \cdot W_{\text{out}})$ memory for the output tensor.
