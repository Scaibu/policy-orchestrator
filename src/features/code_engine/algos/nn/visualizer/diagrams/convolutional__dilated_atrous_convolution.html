◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Dilated (Atrous) 2D Convolution Forward Pass
Require: Input $X \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times H_{\text{in}} \times W_{\text{in}}}$, filter $W \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times C_{\text{in}} \times K_h \times K_w}$, dilation $(r_h, r_w)$, stride $s$, padding $p$, bias $b$.
Ensure: Output $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times H_{\text{out}} \times W_{\text{out}}}$, effective sizes $(K'_h, K'_w)$, output dimensions $(H_{\text{out}}, W_{\text{out}})$.
1 $K'_h \gets r_h(K_h - 1) + 1$, $K'_w \gets r_w(K_w - 1) + 1$
2 $H_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor (H_{\text{in}} + 2p - K'_h) / s \rfloor + 1$, $W_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor (W_{\text{in}} + 2p - K'_w) / s \rfloor + 1$
3 Construct zero-padded tensor $X_{\text{pad}} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times (H_{\text{in}}+2p) \times (W_{\text{in}}+2p)}$
4 Allocate $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times H_{\text{out}} \times W_{\text{out}}}$
5 for $c_{\text{out}} \gets 0 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{out}} - 1$ do
6 for $h \gets 0 \textbf{ to } H_{\text{out}} - 1$ do
7 for $w \gets 0 \textbf{ to } W_{\text{out}} - 1$ do
8 $\text{acc} \gets b[c_{\text{out}}]$
9 for $c_{\text{in}} \gets 0 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{in}} - 1$ do
10 for $i \gets 0 \textbf{ to } K_h - 1$ do
11 $h_{\text{pos}} \gets h \cdot s + i \cdot r_h$
12 for $j \gets 0 \textbf{ to } K_w - 1$ do
13 $w_{\text{pos}} \gets w \cdot s + j \cdot r_w$
14 $\text{acc} \gets \text{acc} + W[c_{\text{out}}][c_{\text{in}}][i][j] \cdot X_{\text{pad}}[c_{\text{in}}][h_{\text{pos}}][w_{\text{pos}}]$
15 $Y[c_{\text{out}}][h][w] \gets \text{acc}$
16 return $\{Y, K'_h, K'_w, H_{\text{out}}, W_{\text{out}}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Exponential Receptive Field Growth under Stacked Dilations
Let an $L$-layer 1D/2D CNN have kernel size $K$ and exponentially increasing dilation rates $r_l = 2^{l-1}$ for $l \in \{1, \dots, L\}$. The total receptive field $\mathcal{RF}_L$ grows exponentially with depth: $$ \mathcal{RF}_L = (K - 1)(2^L - 1) + 1 = \mathcal{O}(K \cdot 2^L) $$ while maintaining linear parameter count $\mathcal{O}(L \cdot K)$.
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ FLOP Count: Performs $C_{\text{out}} H_{\text{out}} W_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K_h K_w$ multiplications (independent of dilation rate $r$).
▸ Total Time Complexity: $\Theta(C_{\text{out}} \cdot C_{\text{in}} \cdot H_{\text{out}} \cdot W_{\text{out}} \cdot K_h \cdot K_w)$ FLOPs.
▸ Storage: $\mathcal{O}(C_{\text{out}} \cdot H_{\text{out}} \cdot W_{\text{out}})$ memory for output feature map.
