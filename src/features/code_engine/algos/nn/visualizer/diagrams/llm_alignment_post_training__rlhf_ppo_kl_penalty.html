In Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), language models are aligned with human intent by maximizing the scalar reward score $R(x, y)$ emitted by a trained reward model. However, unrestricted optimization of $R(x, y)$ induces catastrophic policy drift: the policy exploits holes in the reward model, generating repetitive or ungrammatical text that scores artificially high while ruining coherence. RLHF enforces trust-region stability by penalizing the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence between the active policy $\pi_\theta$ and a frozen reference model $\pi_{\text{ref}}$ (typically the SFT base model). For prompt $x$ and generated response tokens $y = (y_1, \dots, y_T)$, the <strong>Token-Level Penalized Reward</strong> $\tilde{r}_t$ is: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\tilde{r}_t = \begin{cases} -\beta_{\text{KL}} \left( \ln \pi_\theta(y_t \mid x, y_{<t}) - \ln \pi_{\text{ref}}(y_t \mid x, y_{<t}) \right) & \text{if } t < T \\ R(x, y) - \beta_{\text{KL}} \left( \ln \pi_\theta(y_T \mid x, y_{<T}) - \ln \pi_{\text{ref}}(y_T \mid x, y_{<T}) \right) & \text{if } t = T \end{cases}$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM RLHF PPO Step Evaluation and GAE Advantage Computation
Require: Token log-probs $\mathbf{lp}, \mathbf{lp}_{\text{old}}, \mathbf{lp}_{\text{ref}}$, values $\mathbf{V}$, scalar reward $R$, weights $\beta_{\text{KL}}, \gamma, \lambda, \epsilon$
Ensure: Rewards $\tilde{\mathbf{r}}$, total divergence $D_{\text{KL}}$, PPO loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{PPO}}$, value loss $\mathcal{L}_V$, mean advantage $\bar{A}$
1 Allocate $\tilde{\mathbf{r}} \in \mathbb{R}^T, \quad \hat{\mathbf{A}} \in \mathbb{R}^T, \quad \mathbf{Returns} \in \mathbb{R}^T$
2 $total\_kl \gets 0.0$
3 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$ do
4 $kl_t \gets \mathbf{lp}[t] - \mathbf{lp}_{\text{ref}}[t]$
5 $total\_kl \gets total\_kl + kl_t$
6 $\tilde{\mathbf{r}}[t] \gets -\beta_{\text{KL}} \cdot kl_t + (R \text{ if } t == T-1 \text{ else } 0.0)$
7 $last\_gae \gets 0.0$
8 for $t = T-1$ \textbf{downto} $0$ do
9 $next\_v \gets (\mathbf{V}[t+1] \text{ if } t+1 < T \text{ else } 0.0)$
10 $\delta_t \gets \tilde{\mathbf{r}}[t] + \gamma \cdot next\_v - \mathbf{V}[t]$
11 $last\_gae \gets \delta_t + \gamma \cdot \lambda \cdot last\_gae$
12 $\hat{\mathbf{A}}[t] \gets last\_gae, \quad \mathbf{Returns}[t] \gets last\_gae + \mathbf{V}[t]$
13 $surr\_accum \gets 0.0, \quad val\_accum \gets 0.0, \quad low \gets 1 - \epsilon, \quad high \gets 1 + \epsilon$
14 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$ do
15 $ratio \gets \exp(\mathbf{lp}[t] - \mathbf{lp}_{\text{old}}[t])$
16 $clamped \gets \max(low, \min(high, ratio))$
17 $surr\_accum \gets surr\_accum + \min(ratio \cdot \hat{\mathbf{A}}[t], clamped \cdot \hat{\mathbf{A}}[t])$
18 $val\_accum \gets val\_accum + (\mathbf{V}[t] - \mathbf{Returns}[t])^2$
19 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{PPO}} \gets - surr\_accum / T, \quad \mathcal{L}_V \gets val\_accum / T, \quad \bar{A} \gets \frac{1}{T}\sum \hat{\mathbf{A}}[t]$
20 return $(\tilde{\mathbf{r}}, total\_kl, \mathcal{L}_{\text{PPO}}, \mathcal{L}_V, \bar{A})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Analytical Closed-Form Optimal Policy Under KL-Regularized RLHF
The unconstrained optimal policy $\pi^*$ maximizing the expected reward minus KL divergence: $$ \max_\pi \mathbb{E}_{y \sim \pi}[R(x, y)] - \beta_{\text{KL}} D_{\text{KL}}(\pi(y \mid x) \,||\, \pi_{\text{ref}}(y \mid x)) $$ is unique and given analytically by the Gibbs-Boltzmann distribution: $$ \pi^*(y \mid x) = \frac{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y \mid x) \exp\left( \frac{1}{\beta_{\text{KL}}} R(x, y) \right)}{Z(x)}, \quad Z(x) = \sum_{y'} \pi_{\text{ref}}(y' \mid x) \exp\left( \frac{1}{\beta_{\text{KL}}} R(x, y') \right) $$
Non-Negativity of Unbiased Empirical KL Estimator
Let the per-token divergence estimator be defined as $d_{\text{KL}}(t) = \exp(\Delta_t) - \Delta_t - 1$, where $\Delta_t = \ln \pi_{\text{ref}}(y_t) - \ln \pi_\theta(y_t)$. Then $d_{\text{KL}}(t) \ge 0$ for all $\Delta_t \in \mathbb{R}$, with equality if and only if $\Delta_t = 0$.
