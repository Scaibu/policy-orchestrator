◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM StyleGAN Style Modulation and Demodulation Forward Pass
Require: Latent style $\mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_w}$, Affine $\mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times d_w}, \mathbf{b}_A \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}}}$, Weights $\mathbf{W} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times C_{\text{in}} \times K}$, Epsilon $\epsilon > 0$
Ensure: Style scales $\mathbf{s} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}}}$, Demodulated weights $\mathbf{W}'' \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times C_{\text{in}} \times K}$, Norms $\boldsymbol{\sigma} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}}}$
1 $\mathbf{s} \gets \text{zeros}(C_{\text{in}})$
2 for $j \gets 1 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{in}}$ do
3 $s_j \gets b_{A, j} + 1.0 + \sum_{d=1}^{d_w} A_{j, d} \cdot w_d$
4 $\mathbf{W}'' \gets \text{zeros}(C_{\text{out}}, C_{\text{in}}, K), \quad \boldsymbol{\sigma} \gets \text{zeros}(C_{\text{out}})$
5 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{out}}$ do
6 $\text{sum\_sq} \gets 0.0$
7 for $j \gets 1 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{in}}$ do
8 for $k \gets 1 \textbf{ to } K$ do
9 $w_{\text{mod}} \gets s_j \cdot W_{i, j, k}$
10 $\text{sum\_sq} \gets \text{sum\_sq} + w_{\text{mod}} \cdot w_{\text{mod}}$
11 $\sigma_i \gets \sqrt{\text{sum\_sq} + \epsilon}$
12 for $j \gets 1 \textbf{ to } C_{\text{in}}$ do
13 for $k \gets 1 \textbf{ to } K$ do
14 $W''_{i, j, k} \gets (s_j \cdot W_{i, j, k}) / \sigma_i$
15 return $\{\text{style\_scales}: \mathbf{s}, \text{demodulated}: \mathbf{W}'', \text{channel\_norms}: \boldsymbol{\sigma}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Preservation of Unit Output Variance via Weight Demodulation
Let $\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times H \times W}$ denote an input feature map whose channel activations $x_{j, p}$ are mutually uncorrelated with zero mean and unit variance $\mathbb{E}[x_{j, p}] = 0$, $\operatorname{Var}(x_{j, p}) = 1.0$. Let convolution weights be demodulated according to $W''_{i, j, k} = \frac{s_j W_{i, j, k}}{\sqrt{\sum_{j, k} s_j^2 W_{i, j, k}^2}}$. Then the output activation $y_{i, p} = \sum_{j, k} W''_{i, j, k} x_{j, p_k}$ has exact unit variance: $$ \operatorname{Var}(y_{i, p}) = 1.0 $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: Style scale computation: $C_{\text{in}} \times d_w$ operations ($\Theta(C_{\text{in}} d_w)$).
▸ Time Complexity: Modulation and demodulation: $2 C_{\text{out}} \times C_{\text{in}} \times K$ multiplications and additions ($\Theta(C_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K)$).
▸ Time Complexity: Total Time: $\Theta(C_{\text{in}} d_w + C_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K)$ FLOPs.
▸ Space Complexity: Demodulated weight tensor: $\Theta(C_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K)$ floats.
▸ Space Complexity: Auxiliary scale and norm buffers: $\Theta(C_{\text{in}} + C_{\text{out}})$ floats.
▸ Space Complexity: Total Auxiliary Space: $\Theta(C_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K)$.
