◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM MoE Top-$K$ Gating and Dispatch Mask Evaluation
Require: Router logits $\mathbf{Z} \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times E}$, active experts count $k$
Ensure: Selected experts $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{T}}$, gating weights $\mathbf{G}$, dispatch mask $\mathbf{D}$
1 $T \gets \text{rows}(\mathbf{Z}), \quad E \gets \text{cols}(\mathbf{Z})$
2 \textbf{Assert} $1 \le k \le E$
3 Initialize $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{T}}, \mathbf{G}, \mathbf{D}$
4 for $t \gets 0 \textbf{ to } T - 1$ do
5 $\mathbf{z} \gets \mathbf{Z}[t]$
6 Sort expert indices by descending logit value: $\pi_0, \dots, \pi_{E-1}$
7 $\text{top\_indices} \gets [\pi_0, \dots, \pi_{k-1}]$
8 $m \gets \max_{j \in \text{top\_indices}} z[j]$
9 $S \gets \sum_{j \in \text{top\_indices}} \exp(z[j] - m)$
10 $\mathbf{g}_t \gets [\exp(z[j] - m) / S \text{ for } j \in \text{top\_indices}]$
11 $\mathbf{d}_t \gets \text{zeros of size } E$
12 for $j \in \text{top\_indices}$ do
13 $d_t[j] \gets 1$
14 Append $\text{top\_indices}$ to $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{T}}$, $\mathbf{g}_t$ to $\mathbf{G}$, $\mathbf{d}_t$ to $\mathbf{D}$
15 return $\{\text{selected\_experts}: \boldsymbol{\mathcal{T}}, \text{gating\_weights}: \mathbf{G}, \text{dispatch\_mask}: \mathbf{D}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Convex Mixture Invariance
For any token $t$, the active gating weights strictly lie on the $(k-1)$-dimensional probability simplex $\Delta^{k-1}$: $$ \sum_{e \in \mathcal{T}_t} g_{t, e} = 1.0 \quad \text{and} \quad g_{t, e} > 0 \quad \forall e \in \mathcal{T}_t $$ bounding output norms by the convex hull of active expert representations: $\|\mathbf{y}_t\| \le \max_{e \in \mathcal{T}_t} \|\operatorname{Expert}_e(\mathbf{x}_t)\|$.
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Sorting Time Complexity: Finding top-$k$ out of $E$ experts requires $\Theta(E + k \log E)$ operations per token. Across $T$ tokens, time is $\Theta(T E)$.
▸ FLOP Reduction Factor: Computing $k$ experts instead of $E$ reduces feedforward compute by a factor of $E / k$.
▸ Space Complexity: $\Theta(T E)$ for dispatch masks.
