In computational neuroscience and neuromorphic engineering, the <strong>Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF)</strong> model describes the subthreshold membrane voltage $V(t)$ of an electrically polarized neuron governed by the continuous-time linear differential equation: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\tau_m \frac{\mathrm{d}V(t)}{\mathrm{d}t} = - (V(t) - V_{\text{rest}}) + R_m I(t)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Leaky Integrate-and-Fire Dynamic Simulation with Surrogate Gradients
Require: Currents $\mathbf{I} \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$, initial voltage $\mathbf{V}_0 \in \mathbb{R}^N$, leak $\beta \in [0, 1]$, threshold $V_{\text{th}} > 0$, reset mode $\in \{\text{hard}, \text{soft}\}$, surrogate scale $\alpha > 0$
Ensure: Spike trains $\mathbf{S} \in \{0, 1\}^{T \times N}$, final voltage $\mathbf{V}_{\text{final}} \in \mathbb{R}^N$, firing rates $\mathbf{r} \in \mathbb{R}^N$, surrogate grads $\mathbf{G} \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$
1 $\mathbf{V} \gets \operatorname{copy}(\mathbf{V}_0), \quad \text{counts} \gets \mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{Z}^N$
2 Initialize $\mathbf{S} \in \mathbb{Z}^{T \times N}, \quad \mathbf{G} \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times N}$
3 for $t = 0$ \textbf{to} $T-1$ do
4 for $i = 0$ \textbf{to} $N-1$ do
5 $v_{\text{dec}} \gets \beta \cdot \mathbf{V}[i] + \mathbf{I}[t][i]$ \Comment{Leaky Integration}
6 $diff \gets v_{\text{dec}} - V_{\text{th}}$
7 $spike \gets (diff \ge 0.0) \;?\; 1 : 0$ \Comment{Threshold Check}
8 $u \gets \alpha \cdot diff$ \Comment{Arc-Tangent Surrogate Derivative}
9 $grad \gets \frac{1}{\pi} \cdot \frac{\alpha}{1.0 + u^2}$
10 if $spike = 1$} \Comment{State Reset then
11 $\text{counts}[i] \gets \text{counts}[i] + 1$
12 if reset mode is soft then
13 $\mathbf{V}[i] \gets v_{\text{dec}} - V_{\text{th}}$
14 else
15 $\mathbf{V}[i] \gets 0.0$
16 else
17 $\mathbf{V}[i] \gets v_{\text{dec}}$
18 $\mathbf{S}[t][i] \gets spike, \quad \mathbf{G}[t][i] \gets grad$
19 Initialize $\mathbf{r} \in \mathbb{R}^N$
20 for $i = 0$ \textbf{to} $N-1$ do
21 $\mathbf{r}[i] \gets \text{counts}[i] / T$
22 return $(\mathbf{S}, \mathbf{V}, \mathbf{r}, \mathbf{G})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Asymptotic Steady-State Voltage under Constant DC Current
Let a subthreshold LIF neuron receive constant input current $I[t] = I_0$ for all $t \ge 0$, with leak factor $\beta \in [0, 1)$ and initial voltage $V[0] = 0$. If $I_0 < (1 - \beta) V_{\text{th}}$, the neuron will never fire, and its membrane potential converges asymptotically to the steady-state limit: $$ \lim_{t \to \infty} V[t] = \frac{I_0}{1 - \beta} $$
Bounded Mean Firing Rate
Let $T$ denote the total simulation horizon. The temporal mean firing rate $\bar{r}_i = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} S[t, i]$ of any LIF neuron is strictly bounded on the unit interval: $$ 0 \le \bar{r}_i \le 1 $$
