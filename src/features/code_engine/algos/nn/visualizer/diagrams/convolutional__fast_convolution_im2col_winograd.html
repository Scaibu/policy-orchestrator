\subsubsection{im2col Lowering Transformation} Let patch index $m = h_{\text{out}} W_{\text{out}} + w_{\text{out}} \in \{0, \dots, M-1\}$ and channel-spatial index $k = c_{\text{in}} K_h K_w + i K_w + j \in \{0, \dots, K-1\}$. The entry in the lowered matrix is:
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$X_{\text{col}}(m, k) = X_{\text{pad}}(c_{\text{in}}, h_{\text{out}} s_h + i, w_{\text{out}} s_w + j)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM im2col-GEMM Accelerated Convolution Execution
Require: Input $X \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{in}} \times H_{\text{in}} \times W_{\text{in}}}$, weights $W \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times C_{\text{in}} \times K_h \times K_w}$, stride $s$, padding $p$.
Ensure: Output tensor $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{C_{\text{out}} \times H_{\text{out}} \times W_{\text{out}}}$, matrix $X_{\text{col}}$, multiplication count $N_{\text{mul}}$.
1 $H_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor (H_{\text{in}} + 2p - K_h) / s \rfloor + 1$, $W_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor (W_{\text{in}} + 2p - K_w) / s \rfloor + 1$
2 $M \gets H_{\text{out}} \cdot W_{\text{out}}$, $K \gets C_{\text{in}} \cdot K_h \cdot K_w$, $N \gets C_{\text{out}}$
3 Allocate $X_{\text{col}} \in \mathbb{R}^{M \times K}$, $W_{\text{row}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times K}$, $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times H_{\text{out}} \times W_{\text{out}}}$
4 Populate $X_{\text{col}}$ by sliding window unrolling of $X_{\text{pad}}$
5 Flatten $W$ into $W_{\text{row}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times K}$
6 for $m \gets 0 \textbf{ to } M - 1$ do
7 $h_{\text{out}} \gets \lfloor m / W_{\text{out}} \rfloor$, $w_{\text{out}} \gets m \pmod{W_{\text{out}}}$
8 for $n \gets 0 \textbf{ to } N - 1$ do
9 $\text{dot} \gets \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} X_{\text{col}}[m][k] \cdot W_{\text{row}}[n][k]$
10 $Y[n][h_{\text{out}}][w_{\text{out}}] \gets \text{dot}$
11 $N_{\text{mul}} \gets M \cdot N \cdot K$
12 return $\{Y, X_{\text{col}}, N_{\text{mul}}\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Exact Equivalence of im2col-GEMM and Direct Convolution
The matrix product $Y_{\text{col}} = X_{\text{col}} W_{\text{row}}^\top$ is algebraically identical to discrete 2D cross-correlation convolution: $$ Y_{\text{col}}(h_{\text{out}} W_{\text{out}} + w_{\text{out}}, c_{\text{out}}) \equiv Y(c_{\text{out}}, h_{\text{out}}, w_{\text{out}}) $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ im2col Transformation: Unrolling requires $\mathcal{O}(H_{\text{out}} W_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K_h K_w)$ memory writes.
▸ GEMM Multiplication: Multiplying $(M \times K)$ by $(K \times N)$ requires $2 M N K$ FLOPs.
▸ Total Time Complexity: $\Theta(H_{\text{out}} W_{\text{out}} C_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K_h K_w)$ FLOPs.
▸ Matrix Storage: $X_{\text{col}}$ requires $\mathcal{O}(H_{\text{out}} W_{\text{out}} C_{\text{in}} K_h K_w)$ auxiliary floats.
