Multi Head Attention

Let $d_{\text{model}}, h \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$ such that $d_k = d_{\text{model}} / h \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$. Let input domains be $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_q = \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_{\text{model}}}$, $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_k = \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_{\text{model}}}$, $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_v = \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_{\text{model}}}$, and $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}} = (\mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty\})^{N_q \times N_k}$. The <strong>Multi-Head Attention</strong> operator is the parameterized nonlinear mapping: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\operatorname{MultiHead}: \boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_q \times \boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_k \times \boldsymbol{\mathcal{D}}_v \times \boldsymbol{\mathcal{M}} \longrightarrow \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_{\text{model}}}$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Multi-Head Attention Forward Execution
Require: Query $\mathbf{Q} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_{\text{model}}}$, Key $\mathbf{K} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_{\text{model}}}$, Value $\mathbf{V} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_k \times d_{\text{model}}}$
Require: Number of heads $h \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$, optional mask $\mathbf{M} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times N_k}$
Ensure: Output matrix $\mathbf{O} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_q \times d_{\text{model}}}$, head dimension $d_k$, head count $h$
1 $N_q \gets \text{rows}(\mathbf{Q}), \quad d_{\text{model}} \gets \text{cols}(\mathbf{Q})$
2 \textbf{Assert} $d_{\text{model}} \pmod h = 0$ \Comment{Verify exact divisibility}
3 $d_k \gets d_{\text{model}} / h$
4 $\text{head\_outputs} \gets \text{empty list of size } h$
5 for $m \gets 1 \textbf{ to } h$ do
6 $s \gets (m-1) \cdot d_k, \quad e \gets m \cdot d_k$
7 $\mathbf{Q}^{(m)} \gets \mathbf{Q}[:, s:e]$ \Comment{Slice subspace query}
8 $\mathbf{K}^{(m)} \gets \mathbf{K}[:, s:e]$ \Comment{Slice subspace key}
9 $\mathbf{V}^{(m)} \gets \mathbf{V}[:, s:e]$ \Comment{Slice subspace value}
10 $\mathbf{H}_m \gets \text{ScaledDotProductAttention}(\mathbf{Q}^{(m)}, \mathbf{K}^{(m)}, \mathbf{V}^{(m)}, \mathbf{M})[\text{'output'}]$
11 $\text{head\_outputs}[m] \gets \mathbf{H}_m$
12 $\mathbf{O} \gets \text{zeros of shape } (N_q, d_{\text{model}})$
13 for $i \gets 1 \textbf{ to } N_q$ do
14 $\mathbf{O}[i] \gets \text{Concatenate}(\text{head\_outputs}[1][i], \dots, \text{head\_outputs}[h][i])$
15 return $\{\text{output}: \mathbf{O}, \text{head\_dim}: d_k, \text{num\_heads}: h\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
FLOP Invariance of Multi-Head Partitioning
Let single-head attention operate on dimension $d_{\text{model}}$ with sequence lengths $N_q, N_k$. Multi-head attention operating with $h$ heads on partitioned dimensions $d_k = d_{\text{model}} / h$ requires identically the same number of attention FLOPs as single-head attention.
Strict Rank Superiority over Single-Head Attention
Let $N_q, N_k > d_k$ with $d_k = d_{\text{model}} / h < d_{\text{model}}$. A single-head attention operator has maximum output rank $\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{O}_{\text{single}}) \le \min(N_q, N_k, d_{\text{model}})$. However, when sequence lengths are large, single-head attention is constrained by a single softmax matrix $\operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{A}) \le \min(N_q, N_k)$. Multi-head attention allows the output to achieve a maximum rank of: $$ \operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{O}_{\text{multi}}) \le \min\left(N_q, d_{\text{model}}, \sum_{m=1}^h \operatorname{rank}(\mathbf{A}^{(m)})\right) $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: Subspace attention per head: $\Theta(N_q N_k d_k)$ FLOPs.
▸ Time Complexity: Total across all $h$ heads: $h \times \Theta(N_q N_k d_k) = \Theta(N_q N_k d_{\text{model}})$ FLOPs.
▸ Time Complexity: Concatenation: $\Theta(N_q d_{\text{model}})$ memory moves.
▸ Time Complexity: Total Time Complexity: $\Theta(N_q N_k d_{\text{model}})$.
▸ Space Complexity: Multi-head attention buffers $\mathbf{A}$: $\Theta(h N_q N_k)$ floats.
▸ Space Complexity: Concatenated output $\mathbf{O}$: $\Theta(N_q d_{\text{model}})$ floats.
▸ Space Complexity: Total Auxiliary Space: $\Theta(h N_q N_k + N_q d_{\text{model}})$.
