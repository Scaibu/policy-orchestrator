◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM DPO Loss Evaluation and Implicit Reward Tracking
Require: Policy log-probs $\mathbf{lp}_w, \mathbf{lp}_l$, reference log-probs $\mathbf{ref}_w, \mathbf{ref}_l$, scaling factor $\beta$
Ensure: DPO loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}$, implicit rewards $\mathbf{r}_w, \mathbf{r}_l$, mean margin $\bar{\Delta r}$, accuracy $\operatorname{Acc}$
1 Initialize $loss\_accum \gets 0.0, \quad correct \gets 0, \quad margin\_accum \gets 0.0$
2 Allocate $\mathbf{r}_w \in \mathbb{R}^B, \quad \mathbf{r}_l \in \mathbb{R}^B$
3 for $i = 0$ \textbf{to} $B-1$ do
4 $rw \gets \beta \cdot (\mathbf{lp}_w[i] - \mathbf{ref}_w[i])$
5 $rl \gets \beta \cdot (\mathbf{lp}_l[i] - \mathbf{ref}_l[i])$
6 $\mathbf{r}_w[i] \gets rw, \quad \mathbf{r}_l[i] \gets rl$
7 $margin \gets rw - rl$
8 $margin\_accum \gets margin\_accum + margin$
9 if $margin > 0.0$ then
10 $correct \gets correct + 1$
11 if $margin \ge 0.0$ then
12 $loss\_accum \gets loss\_accum + \ln(1 + \exp(-margin))$
13 else
14 $loss\_accum \gets loss\_accum + (-margin + \ln(1 + \exp(margin)))$
15 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}} \gets loss\_accum / B, \quad \bar{\Delta r} \gets margin\_accum / B, \quad \operatorname{Acc} \gets correct / B$
16 return $(\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}, \mathbf{r}_w, \mathbf{r}_l, \bar{\Delta r}, \operatorname{Acc})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Exact Derivation of the DPO Objective from the RLHF Dual
The Direct Preference Optimization loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}$ is an exact mathematical equivalent to maximum likelihood training under the Bradley-Terry preference model parameterized by the optimal RLHF policy.
Vanishing Gradient Property on Saturated Margins
Let the implicit reward margin be $h = \hat{r}_\theta(y_w) - \hat{r}_\theta(y_l)$. As the margin $h \to +\infty$, the gradient norm $\|\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}\| \to 0$ exponentially.

▸ Space Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(N)$ auxiliary memory buffers for intermediate activations.
