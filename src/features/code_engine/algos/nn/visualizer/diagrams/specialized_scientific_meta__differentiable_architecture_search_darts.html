Differentiable Architecture Search Darts

Let a neural network cell be represented as a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of $N$ ordered nodes $\{x_0, x_1, \dots, x_{N-1}\}$, where each directed edge $(i, j)$ represents an information flow and transformation from node $x_i$ to node $x_j$. Let $\mathcal{O}$ denote a discrete candidate set of candidate operations (e.g. $3\times3$ separable convolution, $5\times5$ dilated convolution, max pooling, identity skip, zero operation). To make the categorical choice of operation continuous and differentiable, <strong>Differentiable Architecture Search (DARTS)</strong> relaxes the categorical choice into a continuous softmax mixture over all operations $o \in \mathcal{O}$: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\bar{o}^{(i, j)}(x_i) = \sum_{o \in \mathcal{O}} p_o^{(i, j)} \cdot o(x_i)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Differentiable Architecture Search Edge Relaxation and Discretization
Require: Architecture logits $\boldsymbol{\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}^K$, candidate outputs $\mathbf{U} \in \mathbb{R}^{K \times D}$, temperature $\tau > 0$
Ensure: Relaxed edge output $\bar{\mathbf{y}} \in \mathbb{R}^D$, operation probabilities $\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^K$, winning index $o^*$, entropy $\mathcal{H}$
1 $\alpha_{\max} \gets \max_{0 \le k < K} (\boldsymbol{\alpha}[k] / \tau)$
2 $sum\_exp \gets 0.0$
3 Initialize array $\mathbf{e} \in \mathbb{R}^K$
4 for $k = 0$ \textbf{to} $K-1$ do
5 $\mathbf{e}[k] \gets \exp\left( (\boldsymbol{\alpha}[k] / \tau) - \alpha_{\max} \right)$
6 $sum\_exp \gets sum\_exp + \mathbf{e}[k]$
7 Initialize array $\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^K, \quad o^* \gets 0, \quad \alpha_{\text{best}} \gets -\infty$
8 for $k = 0$ \textbf{to} $K-1$ do
9 $\mathbf{p}[k] \gets \mathbf{e}[k] / sum\_exp$
10 if $\boldsymbol{\alpha}[k] > \alpha_{\text{best}}$ then
11 $\alpha_{\text{best}} \gets \boldsymbol{\alpha}[k], \quad o^* \gets k$
12 Initialize $\bar{\mathbf{y}} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ to zeros
13 for $o = 0$ \textbf{to} $K-1$} \Comment{Convex Combination of Edge Features do
14 $prob \gets \mathbf{p}[o]$
15 for $d = 0$ \textbf{to} $D-1$ do
16 $\bar{\mathbf{y}}[d] \gets \bar{\mathbf{y}}[d] + prob \cdot \mathbf{U}[o][d]$
17 $\mathcal{H} \gets 0.0, \quad \epsilon \gets 10^{-12}$
18 for $k = 0$ \textbf{to} $K-1$} \Comment{Compute Shannon Entropy do
19 if $\mathbf{p}[k] > \epsilon$ then
20 $\mathcal{H} \gets \mathcal{H} - \mathbf{p}[k] \cdot \ln(\mathbf{p}[k])$
21 return $(\bar{\mathbf{y}}, \mathbf{p}, o^*, \mathcal{H})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Convexity of the Relaxed Output Space
Let $\mathcal{U} = \{\mathbf{u}_0, \mathbf{u}_1, \dots, \mathbf{u}_{K-1}\} \subset \mathbb{R}^D$ be the set of activation vectors generated by candidate operations on input $\mathbf{x}$. For any architecture logits $\boldsymbol{\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}^K$ and temperature $\tau > 0$, the relaxed edge output $\bar{\mathbf{y}}$ lies strictly within the relative interior of the convex hull $\operatorname{conv}(\mathcal{U})$: $$ \bar{\mathbf{y}} \in \operatorname{relint}(\operatorname{conv}(\mathcal{U})) $$
Zero-Entropy Convergence in the Low-Temperature Limit
Let $\boldsymbol{\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}^K$ have a unique maximum element $\alpha_{o^*} > \alpha_o$ for all $o \neq o^*$. Then as the softmax temperature parameter $\tau \to 0^+$: $$ \lim_{\tau \to 0^+} p_{o^*} &= 1, \quad \lim_{\tau \to 0^+} p_o = 0 \quad (\forall o \neq o^*) \\ \lim_{\tau \to 0^+} \mathcal{H}(\mathbf{p}) &= 0 $$ }
