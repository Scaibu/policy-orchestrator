Training At Scale

Data Parallelism Allreduce

Data Parallelism (DP) distributes training across $N$ worker nodes by replicating complete model weights $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^P$ across all devices, partitioning the global training mini-batch $\mathcal{B}$ into $N$ distinct local shards $\mathcal{B}_1, \dots, \mathcal{B}_N$, and synchronizing stochastic gradients via an <strong>All-Reduce</strong> collective operation: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\bar{g} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N g_k, \quad g_k = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}_k|} \sum_{x \in \mathcal{B}_k} \nabla_\theta \ell(x; \theta)$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Data Parallel All-Reduce Gradient Averaging
Require: Worker gradients $\mathbf{G} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times P}$, topology $\in \{\text{ring}, \text{tree}\}$, bytes per float $B_{\text{byte}}$
Ensure: Averaged gradient $\bar{\mathbf{g}} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, transferred volume $V$, step count $S$, variance $\sigma^2$
1 Allocate $\bar{\mathbf{g}} \in \mathbb{R}^P, \quad \text{var\_sum} \gets 0.0$
2 for $j = 0$ \textbf{to} $P-1$ do
3 $\text{col\_sum} \gets \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \mathbf{G}[i, j]$
4 $\bar{\mathbf{g}}[j] \gets \text{col\_sum} / N$
5 $\text{var\_sum} \gets \text{var\_sum} + \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} (\mathbf{G}[i, j] - \bar{\mathbf{g}}[j])^2$
6 $\sigma^2 \gets \text{var\_sum} / P$
7 $M \gets P \cdot B_{\text{byte}}$
8 if $N > 1$ then
9 if $\text{topology} == \text{"ring"}$ then
10 $V \gets 2.0 \cdot ((N - 1.0) / N) \cdot M, \quad S \gets 2 \cdot (N - 1)$
11 else
12 $V \gets 2.0 \cdot M, \quad S \gets 2 \cdot \lceil \log_2(N) \rceil$
13 else
14 $V \gets 0.0, \quad S \gets 0$
15 return $(\bar{\mathbf{g}}, V, S, \sigma^2)$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Communication Volume Optimality of Ring All-Reduce
For any distributed system with $N$ worker nodes and full-duplex communication links with bandwidth $B_{\text{bw}}$, the minimum time required to perform an all-reduce on a vector of size $M$ bytes satisfies: $$ T_{\text{allreduce}} \ge 2 \left( \frac{N - 1}{N} \right) \frac{M}{B_{\text{bw}}} $$ and Ring All-Reduce achieves this theoretical bandwidth lower bound.
Variance Reduction Under Distributed Gradient Averaging
Let worker gradients $g_k = \nabla \ell(x_k; \theta)$ be independent unbiased estimators of the true gradient $\mathbb{E}[g_k] = \nabla \mathcal{L}(\theta)$ with covariance $\operatorname{Cov}(g_k) = \Sigma$. The covariance of the all-reduced gradient $\bar{g} = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N g_k$ is strictly reduced by factor $N$: $$ \operatorname{Cov}(\bar{g}) = \frac{1}{N} \Sigma $$
