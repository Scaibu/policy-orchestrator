Physics Informed Neural Networks

Let $\Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^d$ denote a spatial domain with boundary $\partial \Omega$, and let $[0, T]$ denote a temporal domain. Consider a general non-linear partial differential equation (PDE) governing the physical field $u(\mathbf{x}, t)$: {\boldmath } where $\mathcal{N}$ is a differential operator (e.g. Burgers' equation $\mathcal{N}[u] = \frac{\partial u}{\partial t} + u \frac{\partial u}{\partial x} - \nu \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$). A <strong>Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN)</strong> parameterizes the continuous field $u(\mathbf{x}, t)$ with a deep neural network $u_\theta(\mathbf{x}, t)$. The differential operators $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}, \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}, \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$ are evaluated analytically without mesh discretization using reverse-mode automatic differentiation. ALGORITHM Physics-Informed Neural Network Residual Evaluation and Loss Assembly
Require: PDE residuals $\mathbf{r}_{\text{pde}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_f}$, IC predictions $\mathbf{u}_{\text{ic}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_0}$, IC targets $\mathbf{u}_{\text{ic}}^* \in \mathbb{R}^{N_0}$, BC predictions $\mathbf{u}_{\text{bc}} \in \mathbb{R}^{N_b}$, BC targets $\mathbf{u}_{\text{bc}}^* \in \mathbb{R}^{N_b}$, weights $w_f, w_{\text{ic}}, w_{\text{bc}} \ge 0$
Ensure: Composite loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{PINN}}$, PDE loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{pde}}$, IC loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{ic}}$, BC loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{bc}}$, max violation $f_{\max}$
1 $pde\_sq \gets 0.0, \quad f_{\max} \gets 0.0$
2 for $i = 0$ \textbf{to} $N_f - 1$ do
3 $val \gets \mathbf{r}_{\text{pde}}[i]$
4 $abs\_val \gets |val|$
5 if {$abs\_val > f_{\max}$} \State $f_{\max} \gets abs\_val$ \EndIf then
6 $pde\_sq \gets pde\_sq + val \cdot val$
7 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{pde}} \gets pde\_sq / N_f$
8 $ic\_sq \gets \sum_{i=0}^{N_0-1} (\mathbf{u}_{\text{ic}}[i] - \mathbf{u}_{\text{ic}}^*[i])^2$
9 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{ic}} \gets ic\_sq / N_0$
10 $bc\_sq \gets \sum_{i=0}^{N_b-1} (\mathbf{u}_{\text{bc}}[i] - \mathbf{u}_{\text{bc}}^*[i])^2$
11 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{bc}} \gets bc\_sq / N_b$
12 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{PINN}} \gets w_f \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{pde}} + w_{\text{ic}} \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{ic}} + w_{\text{bc}} \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{bc}}$
13 return $(\mathcal{L}_{\text{PINN}}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{pde}}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{ic}}, \mathcal{L}_{\text{bc}}, f_{\max})$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Universal Approximation of Non-Linear Differential Operators by Deep Networks
Let $\mathcal{N}$ be a non-linear partial differential operator satisfying the Cauchy-Kowalevski theorem on compact domain $\Omega \times [0, T]$, and let $u^*$ be the unique classical solution. For any $\epsilon > 0$, there exists a feedforward neural network $u_\theta$ with smooth activations such that: $$ \|u_\theta - u^*\|_{L^2(\Omega \times [0, T])} < \epsilon, \quad \|\mathcal{N}[u_\theta]\|_{L^2(\Omega \times [0, T])} < \epsilon $$
Convergence of the Collocation Residual to Continuous Energy Norm
Let collocation points $\{(\mathbf{x}_i, t_i)\}_{i=1}^{N_f}$ be sampled independently and uniformly from domain $\Omega \times [0, T]$ with volume $V = |\Omega \times [0, T]|$. Then the discrete collocation loss is an unbiased Monte Carlo estimator of the continuous squared $L_2$ residual norm: $$ \lim_{N_f \to \infty} V \cdot \mathcal{L}_{\text{pde}}(\theta) = \int_0^T \int_\Omega |f_\theta(\mathbf{x}, t)|^2 \mathrm{d}\mathbf{x} \mathrm{d}t $$ with standard error decaying as $\mathcal{O}(1 / \sqrt{N_f})$.
