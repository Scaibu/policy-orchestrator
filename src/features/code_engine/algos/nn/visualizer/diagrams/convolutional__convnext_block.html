◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM ConvNeXt Block Forward Evaluation
Require: Input tensor $X \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times H \times W}$, depthwise weights $W_{\text{dw}}$, pointwise weights $W_1 \in \mathbb{R}^{4C \times C}, W_2 \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times 4C}$, LayerNorm parameters $(\gamma_{\text{ln}}, \beta_{\text{ln}})$, LayerScale $\gamma \in \mathbb{R}^C$.
Ensure: Output tensor $Y \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times H \times W}$.
1 $X_{\text{dw}} \gets \text{DepthwiseConv7x7}(X, W_{\text{dw}})$
2 $X_{\text{ln}} \gets \text{LayerNormChannelsLast}(X_{\text{dw}}, \gamma_{\text{ln}}, \beta_{\text{ln}})$
3 $X_{\text{exp}} \gets \text{MatMul}(W_1, X_{\text{ln}})$ \Comment{$C \to 4C$}
4 $X_{\text{act}} \gets \text{GELU}(X_{\text{exp}})$
5 $X_{\text{proj}} \gets \text{MatMul}(W_2, X_{\text{act}})$ \Comment{$4C \to C$}
6 $Y \gets X + \text{diag}(\gamma) X_{\text{proj}}$
7 return $Y$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Layer Scale Gradient Variance Bounds
\label{thm:layerscale} Let the residual transformation be $F(X)$ and let $\gamma_c \sim \mathcal{U}(0, \epsilon)$ with $\epsilon \ll 1$ at initialization. The gradient norm of the residual update satisfies: $$ \mathbb{E}\left[ \left\| \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial X} - \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial Y} \right\|_F^2 \right] \le \epsilon^2 \cdot \mathbb{E}\left[ \left\| \frac{\partial \mathcal{L}}{\partial Y} \right\|_F^2 \right] \cdot \sup \|J_F\|_2^2 $$ guaranteeing stable identity gradient propagation in arbitrarily deep networks ($D \ge 100$).
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: Depthwise $7 \times 7$ Conv: $\mathcal{O}(49 \cdot C \cdot H \cdot W)$.
▸ Time Complexity: LayerNorm: $\mathcal{O}(C \cdot H \cdot W)$.
▸ Time Complexity: Pointwise Expansion ($C \to 4C$): $\mathcal{O}(4C^2 \cdot H \cdot W)$.
▸ Time Complexity: GELU Activation: $\mathcal{O}(4C \cdot H \cdot W)$.
▸ Time Complexity: Pointwise Contraction ($4C \to C$): $\mathcal{O}(4C^2 \cdot H \cdot W)$.
▸ Time Complexity: Total FLOPs: $\mathcal{O}\left( (49C + 8C^2) \cdot H \cdot W \right)$.
▸ Space Complexity: $\mathcal{O}(4C \cdot H \cdot W)$ for forward activation caching.
