Training At Scale

Differentially Private Training Dpsgd

Training large language models on sensitive corpora (e.g., electronic health records, internal corporate code, private user communications) creates severe vulnerability to training data extraction attacks (Carlini et al., 2021). A randomized training algorithm $\mathcal{M}$ satisfies <strong>$(\epsilon, \delta)$-Differential Privacy (DP)</strong> if for any two neighboring datasets $\mathcal{D}, \mathcal{D}'$ differing by at most one individual example ($|\mathcal{D} \triangle \mathcal{D}'| \le 1$), and for every subset of outputs $\mathcal{S} \subseteq \operatorname{Range}(\mathcal{M})$: {\boldmath
◈ PRIMARY MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION & DYNAMIC FLOW KaTeX Verified · Closed-Form
$$\mathbb{P}\left[ \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{D}) \in \mathcal{S} \right] \le e^{\epsilon} \cdot \mathbb{P}\left[ \mathcal{M}(\mathcal{D}') \in \mathcal{S} \right] + \delta$$

◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM Differentially Private SGD (DP-SGD) with Calibrated Gaussian Noise
Require: Per-example gradients $\mathbf{g} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times P}$, clipping norm $C$, noise multiplier $\sigma$, dataset size $N$, step $t$, delta $\delta$
Ensure: Private gradient $\tilde{\mathbf{g}} \in \mathbb{R}^P$, clipping fraction $\mathcal{F}_{\text{clip}}$, mean norm $\bar{\mu}$, estimated privacy loss $\epsilon$
1 $B \gets \operatorname{len}(\mathbf{g}), \quad P \gets \operatorname{len}(\mathbf{g}[0])$
2 Initialize clipped gradients buffer $\bar{\mathbf{g}} \gets [\,], \quad \text{clipped\_count} \gets 0, \quad \text{norm\_sum} \gets 0.0$
3 for $i = 0$ \textbf{to} $B - 1$ do
4 $\|g_i\|_2 \gets \sqrt{\sum_{j=0}^{P-1} g_{i, j}^2}$
5 if $\|g_i\|_2 > C$ then
6 $\text{scale} \gets C / \|g_i\|_2$
7 $\text{clipped\_count} \gets \text{clipped\_count} + 1$
8 $\text{norm\_sum} \gets \text{norm\_sum} + C$
9 Append $[ x \cdot \text{scale} \text{ for } x \in \mathbf{g}_i ]$ to $\bar{\mathbf{g}}$
10 else
11 $\text{norm\_sum} \gets \text{norm\_sum} + \|g_i\|_2$
12 Append $\mathbf{g}_i$ to $\bar{\mathbf{g}}$
13 $\mathcal{F}_{\text{clip}} \gets \text{clipped\_count} / B, \quad \bar{\mu} \gets \text{norm\_sum} / B$
14 Initialize $\tilde{\mathbf{g}} \gets \mathbf{0} \in \mathbb{R}^P$
15 for $j = 0$ \textbf{to} $P - 1$ do
16 $\text{sum}_j \gets \sum_{i=0}^{B-1} \bar{g}_{i, j}$
17 Sample standard normal $\xi_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$
18 $\tilde{g}_j \gets (\text{sum}_j + \sigma \cdot C \cdot \xi_j) / B$
19 Compute RDP cumulative $\epsilon \approx \frac{B}{N} \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2 \cdot t \cdot \ln(1 / \delta)}}{\max(0.1, \sigma)}$
20 return $(\tilde{\mathbf{g}}, \mathcal{F}_{\text{clip}}, \bar{\mu}, \epsilon)$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
$L_2$-Sensitivity of Summed Clipped Gradients
Let $\mathcal{B} = \{x_1, \dots, x_B\}$ and $\mathcal{B}' = \{x_1', \dots, x_B'\}$ be two mini-batches differing by at most one example ($|\mathcal{B} \triangle \mathcal{B}'| \le 2$, with without-loss-of-generality $x_1 \neq x_1'$ and $x_i = x_i'$ for all $i \ge 2$). Let $\bar{g}_i = g_i / \max(1, \|g_i\|_2 / C)$ denote the clipped gradient operator. The $L_2$-sensitivity of the sum query $\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{B}) = \sum_{i=1}^B \bar{g}_i$ is strictly bounded by $2C$: $$ \Delta_2(\mathcal{S}) \triangleq \sup_{\mathcal{B}, \mathcal{B}'} \left\| \mathcal{S}(\mathcal{B}) - \mathcal{S}(\mathcal{B}') \right\|_2 \le 2C $$ If the neighboring definition assumes one record is replaced, $\Delta_2 \le 2C$; if one record is added/removed, $\Delta_2 \le C$.
Privacy Guarantee of the Gaussian Mechanism
Let a query function $f: \mathcal{D} \to \mathbb{R}^P$ have $L_2$-sensitivity $\Delta_2(f)$. For any $\epsilon \in (0, 1)$ and $\delta \in (0, 1)$, the Gaussian mechanism adding noise $\mathcal{Z} \sim \mathcal{N}\left(\mathbf{0}, \sigma^2 \mathbf{I}_P\right)$ satisfies $(\epsilon, \delta)$-Differential Privacy provided the noise standard deviation satisfies: $$ \sigma \ge \frac{\Delta_2(f) \sqrt{2 \ln(1.25 / \delta)}}{\epsilon} $$
