◈ Algorithmic Execution Pipeline
ALGORITHM MoE Load Balancing Loss and Capacity Allocation
Require: Router probabilities $\mathbf{P} \in \mathbb{R}^{T \times E}$, capacity factor $C_{\text{factor}} \ge 1.0$, experts $E$
Ensure: Aux loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}$, expert counts $\mathbf{c}$, dropped count $D$, drop rate $r_{\text{drop}}$
1 $T \gets \text{rows}(\mathbf{P})$
2 $C_{\text{expert}} \gets \lceil (T / E) \cdot C_{\text{factor}} \rceil$
3 Initialize counts $\mathbf{c} \gets \text{zeros of size } E, \quad D \gets 0$
4 for $t \gets 0 \textbf{ to } T - 1$ do
5 $e^* \gets \arg\max_{0 \le e < E} P_{t, e}$
6 if $c[e^*] < C_{\text{expert}}$ then
7 $c[e^*] \gets c[e^*] + 1$
8 else
9 $D \gets D + 1$ \Comment{Buffer overflow: token dropped}
10 $f_e \gets [c[e] / T \text{ for } e \in \{0, \dots, E-1\}]$
11 $P_e \gets [\frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=0}^{T-1} P_{t, e} \text{ for } e \in \{0, \dots, E-1\}]$
12 $\mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}} \gets E \sum_{e=0}^{E-1} f_e \cdot P_e$
13 return $\{\text{aux\_loss}: \mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}, \text{expert\_token\_counts}: \mathbf{c}, \text{dropped\_tokens\_count}: D, \text{token\_drop\_rate}: D / T\}$

★ Theoretical Guarantees & Invariant Bounds
Global Minimum of Load Balancing Loss
\label{thm:moe_aux_min} Let $\mathbf{f}, \mathbf{P} \in \Delta^{E-1}$ lie on the probability simplex. The auxiliary loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}} = E \sum_{e=1}^E f_e P_e$ achieves its global minimum of $1.0$ if and only if both routing probabilities and dispatched tokens are uniformly balanced: $$ f_e = \frac{1}{E} \quad \text{and} \quad P_e = \frac{1}{E} \quad \forall e \in \{1, \dots, E\} \implies \mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}} = 1.0 $$
⚙ Asymptotic Complexity & Hardware Target
▸ Time Complexity: $\Theta(T \cdot E)$ operations to evaluate probabilities and dispatch counts.
▸ Space Complexity: $\Theta(E)$ storage for counts and probability averages.
