\begin{abstract}
Unconstrained sampling from full vocabulary distributions risks selecting improbable tokens from the long tail of the softmax distribution. Top-$k$ sampling removes tail tokens by restricting probability mass exclusively to the $k$ most likely candidates, renormalizing the remaining probabilities. This document specifies the truncation operator, complexity derivations, and worked numerical verification.
\end{abstract}

\section{Mathematical Formulation}

Let $\mathcal{V}^{(k)} = \operatorname{argtopk}(\mathbf{z}, k)$ denote the $k$ largest logits. The renormalized probabilities are:
\begin{equation}
P^{(k)}(v) = \begin{cases}
\frac{\exp(z_v - \max_{j \in \mathcal{V}^{(k)}} z_j)}{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{V}^{(k)}} \exp(z_j - \max_{l \in \mathcal{V}^{(k)}} z_l)}, & \text{if } v \in \mathcal{V}^{(k)} \\
0.0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
